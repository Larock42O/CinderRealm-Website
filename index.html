The Dying World

The chronicles of Dunmere

Dunmere — called Cinderrealm by those who cling to its cooling crust — sits upon a dying subterranean engine: the World-Forge. Below, the fires gutter. Above, an endless frozen abyssal ocean presses in from every horizon. Between the deep heat and the killing cold, civilization holds to the warmth like a traveler to a campfire.

All things in Dunmere exist in one of three states of the Thermal Continuum. The Ember Spark is life and magic — the heat that thinks, the fire that remembers. The Slag is metal and stone and matter — the shaped world, the worked iron, the cities raised by patient hands. And the Cold Ash is decay and frost and hunger — the entropy that waits beyond every wall, wearing the faces of monsters.

Three god-architects raised the world and now quarrel over its ending. Vaelen the Hearth-Father keeps the order of flame and devotion from Ironspire City, wreathed in white gold. Tornil the Slag-Molder guards earth and balance, and the old skill traditions hammered out in forge and field. And Morgast the Ashen Hunger whispers from the frozen Outlands, promising the mercy of the Cold Ash to all who kneel.

You arrive in Emberhold with nothing but a spark. What it becomes — hearthfire or wildfire — is yours to decide.

The Triumvirate

Three architects. One dying engine. Choose whom your spark serves.

🔥

Vaelen

The Hearth-Father · The Spark

God of order, heat, and devotion. Vaelen's white-gold fires burn in every temple brazier from Emberhold to Ironspire City, where the Admin Crown Guard keeps his peace. To serve Vaelen is to hold the line — to keep the hearth lit no matter how dark the ash falls.

Order · Devotion · Ironspire
⛰

Tornil

The Slag-Molder · Balance

God of earth, stone, metals, and balance. Every pick swung in the Glimmer Pits, every blade quenched in Emberhold's forges, honors Tornil. The skill traditions are his liturgy — patience, craft, and the honest weight of iron.

Earth · Craft · Balance
❄

Morgast

The Ashen Hunger · The Cold Ash

God of chaos, entropy, and frost. Morgast rules the lawless Outlands, where the Cold Ash Covenant gathers the desperate and the damned. Its gospel is simple: all fires die, so burn brightest before the dark — or kneel, and let the frost take you gently.

Chaos · Frost · The Outlands
