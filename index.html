Game Updates · 15 October 2026

String Your Own Bows, Answer the Vanguard's Call

Fletchers, rejoice: bowstrings are no longer shop-only. A fibrous plant called ashfiber now grows wild across the realm — gather it, take it to a spinning wheel, and spin your own bowstrings with a bit of Crafting know-how. Fenwick the Bowyer still keeps his shelves stocked in Thornhollow for those in a hurry, but the patient archer now supplies themselves.

Slayer has grown teeth. Four Vanguards now stand across Dunmere, each handing out monster-hunting tasks suited to their tier — from the rats and pests of the lowlands to the horrors that haunt the far reaches. The higher the Vanguard, the deadlier the mark and the richer the reward in Slayer experience and task points. Seek them out, prove yourself, and climb the ranks.

And the roads are safer for it: knights now stand watch at the town gates, guarding the realm's thresholds. They'll not trouble a peaceful traveler — but raise a blade against them and you'll learn why they hold the gate.

The Realm Gets New Faces

The people of Dunmere are getting a whole new look. Shopkeepers, bankers, innkeepers, and blacksmiths in every town now appear as proper, detailed characters instead of the old blocky stand-ins — and shopkeepers will now turn to face you across the counter when you come to trade.

Behind the scenes, the game can now handle more than 200 heroes crowded into one area without breaking a sweat.

And this is only the beginning: new faces are being crafted right now — fishermen, farmers, wizards, town guards, villagers, and more. Keep an eye out over the next couple of days as new characters start popping up around the realm.
