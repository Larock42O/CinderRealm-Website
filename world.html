The Dying World

Dunmere — called Cinderrealm by those who cling to its cooling crust — sits upon a dying subterranean engine: the World-Forge. Below, the fires gutter. Above, an endless frozen abyssal ocean presses in from every horizon. Between the deep heat and the killing cold, civilization holds to the warmth like a traveler to a campfire.

All things exist in one of three states of the Thermal Continuum. The Ember Spark is life and magic. The Slag is metal, stone, and matter. The Cold Ash is decay, frost, and hunger — the entropy waiting beyond every wall.

You arrive in Emberfield with nothing but a spark. What it becomes — hearthfire or wildfire — is yours to decide.

The Triumvirate

🔥 Vaelen

The Hearth-Father · The Spark. God of order, heat, and devotion. His white-gold fires burn in every temple brazier from Emberfield to Ironspire City, where the Admin Crown Guard keeps his peace.

⛰ Tornil

The Slag-Molder · Balance. God of earth, stone, metals, and balance. Every pick swung in the Glimmer Pits honors Tornil — the skill traditions are his liturgy.

❄ Morgast

The Ashen Hunger · The Cold Ash. God of chaos, entropy, and frost. Ruler of the lawless Outlands, where the Cold Ash Covenant gathers the desperate and the damned.

Regions of Dunmere

A fresh chart of the realm is being drawn — check back soon.

Emberfield

The beating heart of the realm — central market town where most heroes wake. Monsters roam the wilds beyond, but no hero hunts another here.

Ironspire City

Great city east of Emberfield. The First Tradekeep and the Admin Crown Guard.

Highcrest Castle

Citadel north of Emberfield, above the heat conduits. The Highcrest Cellars delve below.

Thornhollow

Thorn-choked village in the north, at the edge of the ashen wastes.

Millbrook

Mill town south of Emberfield, on the Millbrook Stream. Goblin trouble in the woods nearby.

Kilnmarch

Eastern frontier town past the Cinderquarry, at the edge of the known world.

Whiteguard Keep

Western coastal fortress on the frozen ocean, facing Saltmere Isle.

Seagaze Harbor

Harbor town south of Whiteguard — docks, fishers, and the boats that will one day sail.

Frosthollow Hills

Snowy highlands in the north-west, above the Canopy Run.

Sunfall Desert & Oasis

South-eastern dunes — bandit camps, the Sunfall Oasis, and the second Tradekeep.

The Gloamfen

Western marsh along the Murkwater. Mosswick village and Lantern Pool at its edge. The wildlife bites.

The Colossus

A fallen giant of stone in the far south. None remember who raised it — or what it guards.

The Outlands & Deep Outlands

PvP — the only dangerous ground. The ashen far-north. Voluntary full-loot PvP — risk and reward scale with depth. Morgast is waiting.

Dungeons of the Deep

⚔ Highcrest Cellars

Beneath Highcrest Castle — torch-lit vaults and the first taste of the deep dark.

⚔ Briar Hollow

A thorn-choked underworld where the roots go deeper than any map.

⚔ Goliath's Vault

The deepest delve — Ember-Core chambers and bosses that do not die quietly.

Skills of the Realm

🔥 Pyrecraft

Master the sacred flame — tend hearths, fire kilns, call down burning judgment.

⚒ Metallurgy

Seven tiers of metal, from slag-iron to white gold. The forge is Tornil's altar.

🙏 Devotion

Prayer and blessing at Vaelen's braziers. Faith, properly tended, is a weapon.

✨ Essencecraft

Draw raw Ember Spark from the world and shape it into runes and relics.

⛏ Excavation

Delve the Glimmer Pits and deep veins. The earth gives — to those who dig.

🎣 Angling

Fish the Murkwater and thawed inlets. Even the Cold Ash leaves the patient fed.

🌿 Herblore

Grind, mix, brew. Gloamfen's swamps grow cures — and poisons.

💀 Slayer

Hunt the Cold Ash's broods for bounty. Some things only die by contract.

…plus Precision, Might, Resilience, Marksmanship, Vitality, Arcana, Culinary, Logging, Artisan, Thieving, Fletching, and Agility.
